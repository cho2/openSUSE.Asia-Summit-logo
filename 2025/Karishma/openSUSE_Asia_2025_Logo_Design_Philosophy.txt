Logo Design Philosophy: openSUSE.Asia Summit 2025

1. Theme:
   The logo embodies the essence of the openSUSE community and the 2025 host country, India.

2. Visual Elements:
   - The Lotus: A national symbol of India, rendered in a symmetrical, stylized design to reflect beauty, balance, and harmony.
   - The Chameleon: The Geeko mascot, integrated with the lotus to signify openSUSE's presence and values.
   - Typography: Serif-style font conveys a professional yet traditional Asian aesthetic.
   - Color: Shades of green align with openSUSE branding, symbolizing growth, openness, and community.

3. Message:
   This logo celebrates open source collaboration in Asia while highlighting India's cultural significance as the summit’s host.
