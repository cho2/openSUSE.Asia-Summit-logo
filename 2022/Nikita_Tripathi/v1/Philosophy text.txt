PHILOSOPHY
I chose Mandala Art, it is a geometric design or pattern that represents the cosmos or deities in various heavenly worlds. 
The art lies withing a Circle since in the ancient Sanskrit language of Hinduism, Buddhism and Jainism, mandala means “circle.” 
A mandala generally represents the spiritual journey, starting from outside to the inner core, through layers which is why the SUSE logo lies in the inner most layer of the design.
The inner circle is adorned with Lotus (India) and Sakura (Japan) motifs, while the outer hand fan (China) has a ripple-like pattern towards the outer half to represent Wi-fi: something everyone will use to connect to the virtual summit.
The final design is kept simple and symmetric with balanced negative spaces to create less noise.
